This site is targeted for data providers, who want to submit Human Data to the ELIXIR-LU Data Repository or to Collaborators at Luxembourg Centre for Systems Biomedicine (LCSB).


In order to use the Data Submission System:


1 Please register with ELIXIR AAI. You will then be able to login to the Submission System using either your Institutional, Google or ORCID account.


2 Please notify your LCSB Collaborator or the ELIXIR-LU Data Steward (by e-mail) about your intention to submit Human Data.


3 You will receive an invite (by e-mail) to login to the Submission System and follow the necessary steps.
